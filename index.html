Taosong Fang 方陶松

I am a Researcher at Dots Studio, Xiaohongshu, where I focus on LLM pretraining and backbone and infrastructure co-design. Beyond these areas, my previous research also covered LLM post-training and ML System for LLM training, inference, and agentic workflows.

I received my master’s degree in 2026 from the Chinese Information Processing Laboratory, Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, co-advised by Prof. Hongyu Lin and Prof. Yaojie Lu. I earned my bachelor’s degree from the School of Software at Dalian University of Technology in 2023. I also collaborate closely with Dr. Zhen Zheng on ML systems research.
